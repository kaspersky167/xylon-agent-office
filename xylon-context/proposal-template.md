# Xylon Devs — Proposal Template Notes

## Structure of a Typical Proposal

1. **Context / What We Heard**
   Briefly reflect back the client's situation and goals. Show we listened.

2. **What We're Proposing**
   Clear statement of the engagement — what we'll do, what we won't do.

3. **Scope**
   Bullet list of deliverables. Keep it tight. Use plain English.

4. **Outcomes**
   What success looks like for the client. 2–4 outcomes max.

5. **Timeline**
   High-level milestones. Start date, key phases, end date.

6. **Investment**
   Fixed price (or milestone breakdown). Payment terms.

7. **Next Steps**
   Single clear CTA — usually "reply to accept" or "book a 15-min call to confirm."

## Tone in Proposals
- Confident, not salesy
- Short paragraphs; avoid walls of text
- Client-first language ("you'll get…" not "we will provide…")
- No filler — every sentence should earn its place

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Over-scoping on paper to justify price
- Vague deliverables that invite scope creep
- Forgetting to include what's explicitly out of scope
- Too much about us, not enough about the client
