# Xylon Devs — Pricing Approach

## General Philosophy
- Value-based over hourly where possible — price the outcome, not the hours
- Transparent scoping upfront so clients know what they're paying for before committing
- No surprise invoices; agreed scope = agreed price
- Flexible to client size — we work with early-stage SMBs and more established businesses

## Rough Pricing Tiers

**Small/starter projects** (e.g. basic Shopify setup, M365 config for a small team)
→ Fixed-price, typically in the low thousands. Clearly scoped, short delivery.

**Mid-range projects** (e.g. Shopify with integrations, M365 + Copilot rollout, automation builds)
→ Fixed or milestone-based. Scoped after discovery call. Usually $3k–$10k+ depending on complexity.

**Ongoing support / retainers**
→ Monthly arrangements for monitoring, updates, helpdesk, or continuous improvement. Custom pricing based on needs.

**Training & workshops**
→ Half-day or full-day sessions. Per-session pricing, can be bundled with project delivery.

## What Affects Price
- Number of users / seats
- Complexity of integrations
- Whether discovery/audit work is needed first
- Urgency and timeline
- Whether ongoing support is included post-launch

## What We Don't Do
- Hourly billing for undefined scope work (we scope first)
- Undercutting quality to win on price
