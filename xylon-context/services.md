# Xylon Devs — Services

## What We Do
Xylon Devs is a Perth-based digital transformation partner that helps Australian SMBs get more from their technology. We combine strategy, implementation, and training so improvements don't just launch — they stick.

## Core Service Areas

**Ecommerce & Shopify**
Build, optimise, and scale Shopify stores. Store builds, redesigns, product/merch setup, checkout and app integrations.

**Microsoft 365 & Business Productivity**
Set up and configure M365 tenants correctly and securely. Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Copilot deployment, AI governance, and staff enablement.

**Cybersecurity & IT**
Security hardening, SMB IT management, risk awareness, and policy. Also covers Copilot access audits and oversharing prevention.

**Automation & Integrations**
Connect tools, remove manual work, and streamline workflows. Venue/POS integrations (hospitality), cross-platform automation, workflow mapping.

**Digital Growth & Marketing**
Lead capture systems, paid and organic growth support, funnel and conversion optimisation.

**Training & Workshops**
Hands-on sessions for owners and teams. Owner intensives, team workshops, tool-specific upskilling (Microsoft, Shopify, AI tools).

## Industries We Work With
Ecommerce brands, professional services, health and allied services, trade and field teams, startups and scaleups, education providers. Also hospitality, NDIS, healthcare, government, legal, and finance for Copilot/security engagements.
