# Xylon Devs — How We Work

## Delivery Model
We follow a consistent 5-step delivery flow across most engagements:

1. **Discover** — Understand the business, audit existing systems, surface risks and gaps
2. **Design** — Map the solution, agree scope, set clear outcomes
3. **Integrate / Build** — Implement the work (Shopify, M365, automation, etc.)
4. **Automate / Optimise** — Layer in efficiency where possible; make the system self-sustaining
5. **Support** — Handover, training, and ongoing availability

## Contact & Intake
- Leads come in via the website contact form
- Form data goes into Google Sheets (source of truth), creates a Google Task, and sends admin notification
- Gemini enriches lead data where available
- Response target: within 24 hours on business days

## Project Start
- Most engagements start within 1–2 weeks of scoping
- We do a discovery/strategy call before proposing scope
- Proposals are written after we understand the business, not before

## Location
- Based in Perth, WA
- Support clients Australia-wide (remote + on-site where needed)

## After Delivery
- Handover includes documentation and/or training
- Ongoing support retainers available
- We stay engaged for system stability as the business evolves
