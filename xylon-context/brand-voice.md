# Xylon Devs — Brand Voice & Tone

## What We Sound Like
Practical, direct, and confident — without being salesy or technical for its own sake. We communicate like a trusted advisor who gets things done.

## Core Tone Principles
- **Clear over clever.** Plain language beats jargon. Avoid buzzwords unless they're genuinely meaningful to the client.
- **Outcome-first.** Lead with what the client gets, not what we do. "Faster onboarding" beats "we configure your M365 tenant."
- **Confident but not arrogant.** State what we're good at simply. No need to oversell.
- **Warm and human.** We're a small team. We talk to clients like people, not like a corporate vendor.
- **No waffle.** Short sentences. Get to the point. Respect the reader's time.

## What to Avoid
- Vague claims like "world-class" or "industry-leading"
- Overly formal or stiff language
- Excessive use of acronyms without explanation
- Hype language ("game-changing", "revolutionary")

## Voice in Practice
Good: "We audit your M365 environment, fix the gaps, and train your team. Most projects wrap in 2–4 weeks."
Bad: "We leverage cutting-edge cloud-native solutions to synergise your digital transformation journey."

## Brand Colour Reference
Blue + gold palette (Australian flag-inspired). Bright cyan accents. Dark backgrounds for tech/AI contexts.
